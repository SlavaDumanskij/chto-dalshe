## Bulbasaur SP

Bulbasaur SP by Sasha Pavljenko.

- Author and original download: https://pavljenko.ru/product/shrift-bulbasaur-sp
- Downloaded file: https://pavljenko.ru/shrift-bulbasaur-sp.woff2
- Current SP license: https://pavljenko.ru/page/sp (version dated 2026-08-28, checked 2026-11-18).
- The author's SP terms permit use in apps and embedding, sharing with the current license link, and format conversion for one's own needs.
- The original WOFF2 was decompressed to OpenType for native loading. Glyphs, character set, and author metadata were not edited.

The typefaces were identified by the user from the Figma design. Font binaries are bundled locally; loading them does not contact a font service.
